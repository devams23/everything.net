Client logs in
    ↓
JWTService creates token
    ↓
Client sends token in header
    ↓
UseAuthentication()
    ↓
JWT Middleware validates:
    - Signature
    - Issuer
    - Audience
    - Expiration
    ↓
If valid → HttpContext.User created
    ↓
UseAuthorization()
    ↓
Role / Policy check
    ↓
Controller executes
